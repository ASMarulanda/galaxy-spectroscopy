# Data provenance, licensing and acknowledgment

This file describes every data product in `data/` — where it came from, what was
changed, what licence applies, and what must be acknowledged when it is reused.
It is the authoritative data record; the README summarises it.

## 1. What is in `data/`

| path | n | what it is | licence position |
|---|---|---|---|
| `data/cases/S01..S10.fits` | 10 | synthetic spectra generated by `tools/generate_synthetic.py` | original work of the author → **CC BY 4.0** (see `LICENSE-content`) |
| `data/cases/R01..R06.fits` | 6 | SDSS DR18 spectra, **redacted** (see §3) | public-domain survey data, modified by the author → **not** covered by the code or content licence; see §2 |
| `data/cases/I01..I04.fits` | 4 | `R01..R04` with one line of exactly known integral added | derivative: author's contribution CC BY 4.0, on top of the SDSS position in §2 |
| `data/truth/truth_synthetic.json` | — | analytic truth for `S01..S10`: exact per-component fluxes, widths, `z`, continuum parameters, seed | author's derived values → CC BY 4.0 |
| `data/truth/truth_injections.json` | — | the exact injected increment per `I` case | author's derived values → CC BY 4.0 |
| `data/provenance/sdss_source_picks.csv` | — | SDSS catalogue rows used to *select* the ten original objects (Phase-1 reconnaissance) | derived from public SDSS catalogues |
| `data/provenance/sha256_data.txt` | — | SHA-256 of the ten raw SDSS files as downloaded | — |
| `data/checksums.sha256` | — | SHA-256 of every file under `data/` | — |

`data/truth/truth_real.json` holds the identity map and the catalogue flux values
for the ten real and injected cases (6,787 bytes; SHA-256 `a57c7d07…`). It is
**not** published. One identity is stated here instead, in §1.1: R06 is SDSS plate
0294, MJD 51986, fibre 0036. The other five remain internal, as do every catalogue
value in that file.

### 1.1 The R06 identity, disclosed

> **R06 = SDSS plate 0294, MJD 51986, fibre 0036** — SDSS DR18 prior-surveys,
> `run2d=26`,
> `spectro/redux/26/spectra/0294/spec-0294-51986-0036.fits`, archive SHA-256
> `12e069c2cceb53459b49204f3f508aef3acdd704fd14a136c0b6449e8a118554`.

**Why it is disclosed.** Two reasons, in order of weight.

*It was already derivable.* §6 finding 3 recorded that the identities of five of
the six real cases are reachable from published files. Re-checked at the V0.3
review: matching `catalogue_z` in
`validation/results/tables/archival_redshift.csv` against `zspec` in
`data/provenance/sdss_source_picks.csv` gives a **unique** match for R01, R02, R03,
R04 and R06. Only R05 is genuinely unidentified. Describing a derivable mapping as
"withheld" was bookkeeping, not secrecy.

*V0.3 ships a download workflow.* Once `tools/download_sdss.py` fetches this
spectrum in one command, anyone who had inferred the mapping would be one command
from the archive file. Leaving the statement implicit while making the checkers
effortless would misrepresent what was being protected. The source is public
observational data — SDSS Legacy spectra are in the public domain (§2) — so this
discloses nothing that was ever private to the author.

**What it establishes.** Identification only: that the case file is this archive
spectrum and no other. It is checkable, and it was checked — the six `COADD`
columns of `data/cases/R06.fits` are **bit-identical** to the archive file's, and
`gspec measure` on the freshly downloaded spectrum reproduces the R06 row of
`validation/results/golden_reference.csv` in every field. §3 records why the other
five cases cannot be checked this way.

**What it does not establish.** It is not validation of our fitted numbers. Those
are judged against analytic synthetic truth (S01–S10), the differential injection
identity (I01–I04), and an independent implementation — never against this object's
catalogue values, which are other groups' measurements of the same photons:

| | gspec | SDSS pipeline (`LINEAREA`) | MPA-JHU |
|---|---|---|---|
| redshift | 0.0812015 ± 0.0000355 | 0.08116169 (catalogue) | — |
| Hα | 9.88 ± 5.03 — non-detection | 14.12 | 16.24 ± 1.88 |
| Hβ | 56.3 ± 449.3 — non-detection | −0.62 | 2.70 ± 1.79 |
| [N II] 6583 | 8.85 ± 1.80 | 10.30 | 8.98 ± 1.70 |
| [O III] 5007 | 1.63 ± 2.56 — non-detection | 1.98 | 2.47 ± 1.92 |

The redshift agrees with the survey's to 1.1 σ (11 km s⁻¹). The fluxes agree where
a line is detected and mean nothing where it is not: for this object's Hβ, neither
the survey pipeline nor the MPA catalogue measures a line either, which is the
honest reading of a spectrum with median S/N ≈ 7. Knowing *which object* a case is
says nothing about whether the measurement is any good, and on this object the
measurement is not good for Hβ — see `docs/limitations.md` L2.

**What remains withheld.** The identities of the other five cases (R05 in
particular cannot be derived from published files), and every catalogue value in
`truth_real.json`: 24 pipeline flux measurements and 24 MPA fluxes with their
errors. None of them is needed by the workflow, and publishing them would present
other groups' fits as reference truth standing beside ours.

## 2. The SDSS material

The six real cases and the four injection bases are drawn from the
**SDSS-I/II Legacy** spectroscopic survey, reduced with `run2d=26`, served by
the SDSS DR18 Science Archive Server:

```
https://data.sdss.org/sas/dr18/prior-surveys/sdss2-dr8-sdss/spectro/redux/26/
        spectra/{plate:04d}/spec-{plate:04d}-{mjd:05d}-{fiber:04d}.fits
```

The plate is zero-padded to four digits in the **directory** as well as in the file
name. Earlier versions of this page showed the directory unpadded; that path returns
404 for plate 294, while the padded one returns 200. Corrected in V0.3, whose
`tools/download_sdss.py` builds the padded form and validates every identifier
before any URL exists.

**Licence position, as stated by SDSS** (retrieved 2026-11-13):

> "All SDSS data released in our public data releases are considered in the
> public domain."
> — SDSS, *Image Use Policy* / *Data Use Policy*, https://www.sdss.org/collaboration/

SDSS **images** are additionally offered under CC BY; that does not apply to
spectra, which fall under the public-domain statement above.

**One caveat that is recorded rather than dropped.** The DR1-era SkyServer data
page carries the sentence:

> "Data from the SDSS public archive may not be used for any commercial
> publication or other commercial purpose except with explicit approval by the
> Astrophysical Research Consortium (ARC)."
> — https://skyserver.sdss.org/dr1/en/credits/datause.asp

Current SDSS pages do not repeat this sentence. It does not constrain the
non-commercial use made of the data here, but a reader who wants to reuse these
files commercially should treat it as unresolved and contact ARC.

### Retrieving the archive files

Nothing under `data/raw/` is distributed, and that path is git-ignored: the archive
files carry `SPECOBJ` and `SPZLINE` (the survey pipeline's own redshift and line
fluxes) and would give away the answers the case files were redacted to remove.
`tools/download_sdss.py` fetches them by identifier, verifies size and SHA-256, and
converts them; the download workspace it writes is local and is never committed.

## 3. The redaction record

The case files are **not** the archive files. `tools/make_injections.py` removes,
from every real case, everything that would let a reader answer without
measuring:

| removed | where it lived | why |
|---|---|---|
| `SPZLINE` extension | 4th HDU | contains the SDSS pipeline's own line fluxes — the literal answers |
| `SPECOBJ` extension / `SPECOBJ.Z` | 3rd HDU, header | catalogue redshift, subclass, plate/MJD/fibre |
| `PLATE`, `MJD`, `FIBERID`, `RA`, `DEC` | headers | object identity → a catalogue lookup or a memory shortcut |
| `sky` extension | HDU | sky-subtraction model; not needed to measure |
| `model` extension | HDU | the pipeline's own fitted model — a free starting point |

**Kept:** the calibrated co-added spectrum (`COADD`, six columns), `BUNIT`,
`VACUUM`, an opaque `CASEID`, and a `PROVEN` keyword recording which family the
file belongs to.

Consequence to be honest about: the case files are a *derived product*. They
reproduce the SDSS flux calibration and noise, but a reader cannot check them
against the archive without the mapping. For R06 that mapping is now published
(§1.1), so that one case can be verified end to end against the archive file; for
the other five it cannot, and the mapping stays internal.

## 4. How the synthetic data were made

`tools/generate_synthetic.py`, seed `20261106 + 10007 × case index`, so every
case is a pure function of its index and the whole set is reproducible bit for
bit on any machine with numpy.

* Continuum: three power laws with Dirichlet-drawn amplitudes and sorted slopes,
  multiplied by a 12-line stellar absorption pattern at a single scale factor
  per case, `HB_DEPTH = 0.11`.
* Emission: Gaussians whose **integral** is the declared flux
  (`amp = flux / (σ_obs √(2π))`), so the truth is arithmetic, not a fit.
* Noise: `σ(λ) = continuum(λ) / SNR₆₀₀₀/(λ/6000)^0.4`.
* Self-test: re-integrates every component on the pixel grid; worst relative
  error 8.8e-9 over the ten cases.

I01–I04 are produced by `tools/make_injections.py`: the copy and its base share
one noise realisation, so for any measurement operator M that is linear enough
over the increment, `M(copy) − M(base) = F_injected` exactly.

## 6. Provenance audit (Stage 3)

Every derived truth value in `data/` was re-checked against the raw archive files
and the public catalogues they came from. The audit ran with the raw files
available (they are not redistributed here; a download workflow ships in v0.3).

| check | result |
|---|---|
| raw files vs `data/provenance/sha256_data.txt` | **10 / 10 exact** |
| `truth_real.json` plate/MJD/fibre vs the `SPECOBJ` extension | **exact, 6 / 6** |
| `truth_real.json` `z` and `SUBCLASS` vs `SPECOBJ` | **exact, 6 / 6** |
| `truth_real.json` `lines[*].pipe` vs `SPZLINE.LINEAREA` | **exact, 24 / 24 values** |
| `truth_real.json` `lines[*].mpa` vs the MPA-JHU catalogue table | **exact, 24 / 24 values** |
| case FITS `flux` vs the raw `COADD.flux` | **byte-equal, 6 / 6** — the redaction changed no flux value |
| `truth_injections.json` increments vs 0.40 × the pipeline flux | **exact, 12 / 12** |
| `S01`–`S10` vs `tools/generate_synthetic.py` | **bit for bit, 10 / 10** (`tests/test_generator.py`) |

So every number in `data/` is reconstructible from public SDSS products: the
pipeline fluxes from `SPZLINE` in the archive file, the MPA-JHU fluxes from that
value-added catalogue, the injections as an arithmetic 40 % of the pipeline flux,
and the synthetic cases from a documented seed.

### Three findings a reader should know

1. **The columns of `data/provenance/sdss_source_picks.csv` are Portsmouth
   fluxes, not the MPA-JHU values used in the truth manifests.** Verified: its
   `Flux_Ha_6562` equals the Portsmouth `por_Ha` value exactly for every case it
   covers, and it does **not** equal either the pipeline or the MPA value — for
   R01 it is 2145.3 against 812.8 (pipeline) and 700.2 (MPA), a factor 2.6–3.1.
   This is the same anomaly recorded in the project's history as "Portsmouth Hα
   ≈ 2.4× high", now localised to a column name that does not say so. Read the
   table as the Phase-1 *reconnaissance* record, not as a flux reference.
2. **That table covers 8 of the 10 objects.** `968/11` (case **R05**, the
   broad-line AGN) and `1238/229` (an object not used in this release)
   are absent. Nothing in the repository depends on it, but a reader should not
   assume it is complete.
3. **Withholding `truth_real.json` does not withhold the identities or the
   redshifts, because they are already published elsewhere in the repository:**
   `data/provenance/sdss_source_picks.csv` carries plate/MJD/fibre for
   **5 of the 6** real cases (R01–R04 and R06), and
   `validation/results/tables/archival_redshift.csv` carries the archive redshift
   for **all six**. What `truth_real.json` would add publicly is the identity of
   R05 and the pipe/MPA flux values quoted in the validation tables. That is a
   decision for the release review, not a data-protection question. **Resolved at
   the V0.3 review:** the R06 identity is now stated explicitly in §1.1, on the
   grounds that it was derivable anyway and that a download workflow makes the
   check trivial; the other five identities and all catalogue values remain
   internal.

## 7. Storage precision (a limitation, not a detail)

All `COADD` columns are stored as **float32** (`format='E'`). The spectra
therefore carry ~1e-7 relative quantization, and no reproducibility claim
tighter than ~1e-6 relative can be tested against these files. The pipeline
computes in float64; the input precision is the binding constraint.
